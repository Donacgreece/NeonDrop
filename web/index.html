About NEON DROP

NEON DROP is a free mobile browser arcade game by Donacgreece. Move a glowing ball through increasingly fast neon gates using smooth one-finger drag controls. Each player begins with three lives. The game requires no account or download and stores the Top 10 scores locally on the player's device.
